Qibla Finder

The direction of the Kaaba in Makkah from wherever you are, as degrees from north, with a live compass on phones. Your location is used only on this device.

—

On a phone: hold it flat, top edge pointing ahead. Move it in a figure-8 if the reading looks wrong.

Your location

Degrees are from true north. A magnetic compass points to magnetic north, which can differ by a few degrees depending on where you are.
